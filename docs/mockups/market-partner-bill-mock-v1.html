Deep audit — market side ki asli halat (production, 5 Sep 08:10)

  1. Data shunya hai. 2 load (dono OPEN, koi bid nahi), 0 bid, 0 settlement, 0 market vehicle, 0 fleet partner (18 vendor sab SERVICE — pump/tyre), 0 market ledger, 0 bazaar voucher. Model sirf code me hai; asli paise par kabhi chala nahi.
  2. Partner ko TDS kahin nahi kat-ta. Advance (BZADV) aur balance (BZBAL) PAYMENT voucher poora gross post karte hain. Tax engine me 194C ka niyam (1% individual / 2% firm / 20% bina PAN, ₹30,000 single / ₹1,00,000 FY threshold, 194C(6) declaration) likha hai par bazaar use bulata hi nahi.
  3. TDS post ho bhi nahi sakta. Migration 129 ka segment guard: BAZAAR_* voucher sirf "Market Fleet %" group ya Bank/Cash chhoo sakta hai. "TDS Payable" Duties & Taxes me hai → FLEET_CROSSOVER (P0403). Bill ke liye naya group Market Fleet Duties & Taxes + ledger Market Fleet TDS Payable 194C banana hoga.
  4. Partner master me TDS ke liye field nahi. vendors me pan_no, bank_account, ifsc_code hain; par individual/firm (1% ya 2%) aur 194C(6) declaration ka flag nahi — bina iske rate anuman hoga, aur anuman post nahi karte.
  5. Paisa load-wise, haath se. 90% advance loading par, balance POD verify ke baad — har load par admin alag voucher. Koi 15-din ka chakra nahi; partner apne app me sirf khata (vendor_txns) dekhta hai, statement nahi.
  6. Books sahi disha me hain. Lock par Dr Market Fleet Freight Cost / Cr Market Partner (BZLOCK); POD par Dr Market Debtors / Cr Market Fleet Freight Income (BZINC); margin kabhi teesri entry nahi. Bill inhi par ek statement hai — kuch dobara post nahi hota.
  7. Settlement par company_id staff set karta hai; bina firm ke koi paisa nahi chalta (CHECK). Bill bhi firm-wise banega — jaise own/attached.

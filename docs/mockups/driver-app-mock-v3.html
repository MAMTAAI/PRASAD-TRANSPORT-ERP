What is on the home screen now

  • Top half = live map. Blue line is the route Barauni → Ranchi. Grey dashed line under it is the return leg of a round trip. 🟢 loading point, 🔴 unloading point, 🚚 the truck where the phone's GPS says it is, with speed. The card on the map gives km left and time left.
  • Bottom half = the buttons. Green POD / Bilty full width. Diesel and Digital Locker side by side. Black bar calls the dispatch mobile in one tap.
  • Market (hired) driver sees the map and the POD button only. No diesel, no locker.
  • Digital Locker replaces "My papers". A document the office has approved shows View and PDF buttons. A missing or rejected one shows a camera button. One under review shows only "office is checking".
  • No typing anywhere. Tap → camera → send. Office reads numbers from the photo with the OCR already live.
  • Tap targets 46 px or taller, button text 16–28 px. Green = send paper, amber = diesel, blue = locker, red = something to fix.

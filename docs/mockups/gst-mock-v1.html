How the number is built
Reverse charge — IOCL, BPCL, HPCL, Aadhar Green and every other registered or corporate customer: the invoice shows 5% GST, the recipient pays it, it never enters our receivable (Notification 13/2017).
Forward charge — customers a person switched to FORWARD: we add 5% (no credit) or 12% (with credit) to the invoice and the receivable.
Input credit — GST on spares, tyres, batteries, insurance, repairs and goods carriages. Claimable only under the 12% option; under reverse charge it stays on record as blocked (Sec 17(3)). Toll is exempt, diesel is outside GST — both go to GSTR-3B table 5.
Net payable = output tax − eligible credit, set off IGST → CGST → SGST (Rule 88A).

✂️ TDS Management v2 — both directions, per firm, per quarter

Two books of TDS. On us: what IOCL, BPCL and HPCL withhold from our freight (Section 194C, 2%) — an asset we claim against income tax, provable only against Form 26AS and Form 16A. By us: what we must withhold when we pay attached vehicle owners, fleet partners and contractors — a liability we deposit by the 7th of the next month and report quarterly in Form 26Q. Today the ERP records neither properly: the screen holds two hand-typed rows.

How the liability is built

BaseThe 15-day bill's commission (our charge to the attached owner) — not the freight, which is the owner's — plus any other contractor payment the tax engine marks 194C.
Rate1% individual/HUF with PAN · 2% firm/company · 20% when no PAN · nil with a 194C(6) declaration (≤10 goods carriages + PAN). Thresholds ₹30,000 single / ₹1,00,000 in the FY.
WhenAt the earlier of credit (bill approval) or payment. Deposit by the 7th of the next month (30 April for March) — challan ITNS 281 under the firm's TAN.
LedgerApprove: Dr Vehicle Owner / Cr TDS Payable 194C (per firm, Duties & Taxes). Challan: Dr TDS Payable 194C / Cr Bank — and the bank line auto-tallies to it.
ReturnForm 26Q per quarter: Q1 by 31 Jul · Q2 by 31 Oct · Q3 by 31 Jan · Q4 by 31 May. Form 16A to each owner within 15 days of the return.
Blocked today by two masters, not by code: commission rates for 16 attached lorries (Commission Master) and PAN + entity type per owner. Both become one screen: the Deductee master.

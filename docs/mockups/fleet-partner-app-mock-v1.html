What the fleet partner gets

  • Home = "which of my trucks are earning right now". Three counters (on trip / free / awaiting approval), a "Market money" card (advance received, balance due, deposit held, month total) and the list of trucks on trip with the stage each one is at — the same 7-stage settlement pipeline the desk uses, in plain words.
  • One button per stage, nothing else. AWAITING_CONFIRM shows "✅ Confirm" with the deadline; CONFIRMED shows "🚚 Assign truck"; ADVANCE_PAID shows "📷 POD photo". After POD the balance waits for the office to verify — the card says so.
  • Money is honest: advance is 90 % of the award (the real default), balance releases only on POD_VERIFIED (the real gate), deposit shown as "held by office" with its BZDEP reference. Every paid line carries its TARA reference so a partner can match it to the bank.
  • Trip history = month groups with per-month totals, truck, lane, award, and a SETTLED pill; statement PDF from the existing endpoint.
  • My trucks = the market_vehicles list with System Active / Pending approval / Blocked, expiry warnings (insurance, fitness, permit), and "add a truck" that lands as PENDING APPROVAL — Milan/OCR reviews the papers exactly as for drivers.
  • Loads and blind bidding (L1 rank) stay as today, one screen, since they already work — the focus here is the truck-on-trip and money views.

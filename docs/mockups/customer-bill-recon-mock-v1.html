Jhande ka niyam: Paid = unka gross − TDS = paid (±₹1). Short-paid = penalty/shortage kaat kar diya (maan lo → Shortage & Penalty; ya dispute). Missing freight = hamara trip unke bill/advice me hai hi nahi — yahi asli nuksaan hai, dispute ke saath IOCL ko list jaati hai. Pending = delivered, abhi koi advice nahi (umar dikhti hai). Unmatched = unki line, hamara trip nahi (aaj 91 no-trip + 72 location) — trip jodo. Kataauti = CCMS diesel / toll / misc — kharch ke khaate me, freight se ghataya nahi jaata. Aaj yeh sab tools/iocl_recon ka pipeline pehle se nikalta hai; naya sirf yeh ki bill par dikhega aur bill ka status isse badlega.

  • 1Trip complete (AC5 / loading entry) — customer, branch, qty, RTKM, rate trip par. Bina customer/branch ke trip mapping desk me.
  • 21 aur 16 tareekh 03:00 — TARA har customer ka CB- bill draft (contract customer: 1 ko mahine ka), branch-wise, IOCL format.

  • 4Unka bill / advice aaya — pipeline padhta hai, trip-wise milaan, jhande, bill PART-PAID/PAID; "Post receipt" se bank + TDS + kataauti ke khaate.
  • 5Dispute — missing/short list IOCL ko, agle advice tak khula.

YES se pehle 5 baatein — inhi par build alag hoga

  1. Bill = customer (parent) × firm ki books × pakhwada, branch andar. 417 purane per-location bill waise hi rehte hain; naya roop naye cycle se (ya chahein to Apr se dobara draft — purane cancel karke).

  4. 100 bina-customer trip aur 11 naam ke roop — mapping desk par staff tay kare; system anuman nahi lagata.
  5. Contract (Aadhar) mahine ka bill 1 tareekh ko, detention 0 jab tak plant reporting stamp nahi. Market customer ka bill POD par load-wise (Load Bazaar) — is screen me sirf dikhega.

② Statements & auto-tally — upload, parse, tally, post

Drop the SBI statement here — PDF (as the bank e-mails it), CSV or XLSX. Password-protected PDFs accepted (password kept only for that upload).
Account is read from the document (00000030178368490 → SBI (8490), Prasad). Lines already imported are skipped by their hash; a month re-uploaded is harmless.
1 · ParseTxn date, value date, narration, ref/UTR, debit, credit, balance. Counterparty and channel (UPI / INB / NEFT / RTGS / cheque / cash) extracted from the narration.
2 · Tally (TARA)UTR → IOCL advice · amount+date → book entry · payer name → customer bill · payee name → owner / vendor / driver · pattern → EMI, FASTag, card, charges · own firms → inter-firm.
3 · PostExact (UTR or unique amount+party) posts the voucher in the account's firm and clears the due. Everything else → Staff Action. Never posts twice (deterministic ref BANK-<account>-<line hash>).
4 · LearnA staff decision on a counterparty becomes a rule (name → party, pattern → ledger). Next month the same line auto-tallies.

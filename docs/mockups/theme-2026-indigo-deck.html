Indigo Deck
a control room, not a black screen

Today every deck sits on #020617 — near-black, so the panels, the borders and the numbers all read as one flat sheet. Indigo Deck lifts the ground to a deep navy, tints the panels above it, and gives each status its own live colour: cyan for tracking, green for active, amber for anything waiting on a person. Flip the switch and watch the same Command Deck change.

What actually changes

Seven values. Every screen reads them through a token, so nothing is re-drawn by hand.

App ground
#0a1024#020617

Deep navy with a cyan wash top-right and violet bottom-left. Reads as blue, not as an off monitor.

Panel
#121c38#0f172a

Lifted and tinted so a card is visibly a card before you look for its border.

Raised / inset
#18244a#1e293b

Chips, table hover, money boxes — one step above the panel, never below it.

Hairline
#27395f#334155

Blue-tinted, and it brightens to #3d548a on hover so a card answers the pointer.

Live tracking
#22d3ee#38bdf8

Glowing cyan. GPS, live pills, the active nav row — anything moving right now.

Active / settled
#2fe39b#10b981

Neon green, brighter than the old emerald so money that landed reads at a glance.

Waiting on a person
#ffb224#f59e0b

Vibrant amber on every queue with work in it — the desk chips light up on their own.

Blocked / critical
#ff6b81#ef4444

Rose rather than fire-engine red — carries on navy without vibrating against it.

Where it lands

Token-level, so one edit retints whole screens rather than one card at a time.

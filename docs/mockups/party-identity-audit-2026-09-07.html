5 Design — aapka niyam, system ki bhaasha mein

Aapne jo kaha wo ek line mein: party company se upar hai. Company gaadi par likhi jaati hai, maalik par nahi. Isliye Sandeep Kr Prasad ki ek hi ID rahegi, chahe wo Prasad Transport mein 13 gaadi lagayein aur Jaiswal Enterprise mein 2.

  1. Naam milane ka niyam sudharo

    party_key() ab shuru ka M/S / MESSRS hatayega aur KR / KUM → KUMAR maanega. Purana LTD/PVT/AND hatana waisa hi rahega.

  2. Gaadi ke maalik ka asli master banao

    Naya table vehicle_owners — ID, naam, PAN, phone, khata. Uski party_key par UNIQUE — poore system mein, company-wise nahi. Yahi aapka niyam hai. vehicles.owner_id us ID par jayega. Purana owner_name dikhane ke liye rahega, pehchaan ID hogi.

  3. 6 spelling → 5 asli maalik

    GAUTAM PRASAD aur M/S GAUTAM PRASAD ek ho jayenge (6 trucks ek maalik par). Jaiswal Enterprise ka khata bhi ban jayega — abhi hai hi nahi.

  4. Darwaza band karo — dobara duplicate ban hi na sake

    vendors, customers, companies aur naye vehicle_owners — chaaron par UNIQUE on party_key(naam). Iske baad "Sandeep Kr Prasad" type karne par system usi purani ID par le jayega, nayi ID banegi hi nahi.

  5. Naya maalik apne aap sahi ID par jude

    Ek trigger — gaadi par naya owner_name likha to system pehle purani ID dhoondhega; milti hai to usi se jodega, nahi milti to nayi party banayega. Chaaron rasta (Fleet master, importer, driver app, agents) ek hi darwaze se guzrenge.

  6. Jo paise wale hain wo aapke paas aayenge

    NIRMALA (0 paisa) apne aap judega. JOHN N WELL (₹1,858) aur 3 RAJESH KUMAR Approval Desk par task banenge — dono taraf ka hisaab saamne, aur aap "Jodo" ya "Alag rehne do" dabayenge.

6 Ek baat saaf kar dena chahta hoon

System ki 45 tables mein party ka naam khuli text ki tarah pada hai — trips.driver_name, fuel_entries.vendor_name, loan_master.owner_name, aur aise hi 40 aur. In sabko ek saath ID par le jaana mahino ka kaam hai aur har money screen ko chhoo jayega.

Meri salaah: abhi Phase 1 karein — master + darwaza. Isse (a) aaj ke duplicate khatam, (b) naya duplicate ban hi nahi sakta, (c) Gautam Prasad wali gaadi apne hisaab mein aa jayegi. Baaki 45 text columns wahi party_key() se milte rahenge jaise aaj milte hain — bas ab sahi niyam ke saath. Phase 2 (har table par ID) alag se, aapke kehne par.

Bhejne se pehle 3 baatein tay kar lein

  1. KR = KUMAR maan lein? Isse "SANDEEP KR PRASAD" aur "SANDEEP KUMAR PRASAD" ek ho jayenge. Par agar kabhi sach mein do alag aadmi hon jinme ek ka naam KR ho — wo bhi ek maan liye jayenge. Aapke fleet mein aisa koi nahi hai (maine dekha), par niyam aage ke liye hai.
  2. Owner ki ID company se upar — Sandeep Prasad Transport mein bhi aur Jaiswal mein bhi, ek hi ID. Maine aapki baat aise hi samjhi hai. Confirm kar dein.
  3. Phase 1 abhi, Phase 2 baad mein? Ya aap chahte hain ki main saari 45 tables ek saath ID par le jaun (bahut bada kaam, har money screen chhu jayegi)?

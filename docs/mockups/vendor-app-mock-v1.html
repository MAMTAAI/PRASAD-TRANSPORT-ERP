Interactive prototype · PAWAN SERVICE STATION (HSD pump)

What the vendor gets

  • Home = money first. A "Payment status" card exactly like the driver's allowance card: bills raised, approved, paid, and the balance the office still owes — the number a pump owner actually opens the app for.
  • Loading slip = one photo. Camera opens, OCR (BHUVANESHWARI) reads truck no, litres and amount off the slip, the vendor only confirms. Zero typing on a good photo.
  • Expense bill = same camera, different type chip (HSD / Tyre / Maintenance / Toll / Other — the real expense_type list). Lands in the office's Pending Expenses queue as PENDING; nothing is ever "done" on the phone.
  • Every bill shows a 3-step stepper: Submitted → Approved → Paid (posted by TARA, voucher no shown). Rejected bills carry the office's reason and a "send again" button.
  • Payments tab = the vendor's khata: payments received (NEFT / UTR), bills received, running balance, and a Statement PDF — the same PDF the office already serves at /portal/vendor/statement.pdf.
  • Hindi first with an EN toggle, 46 px targets, emoji icons, black call bar to the office — carried over from the approved driver app.

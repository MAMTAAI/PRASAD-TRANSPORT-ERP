Why a crosshair on the phone and a draggable pin on the PC. Dragging a 30-px pin with a thumb is the single most-missed target on a touch screen. Every mapping app people already use — Uber, Swiggy, Ola — moves the map under a fixed centre crosshair instead. The PC keeps the draggable pin because a mouse is precise. Same component, one prop.

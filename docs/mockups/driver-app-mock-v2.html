Interactive prototype · tap a tile → camera → send → done

Rules the mock follows

  • Three buttons, nothing else. POD / Bilty, Diesel Slip, KYC papers. Every button fills a third of the screen and reads in Hindi first, English under it.
  • No typing anywhere. Tap → camera opens → send. The office reads amounts, Aadhaar, account and IFSC from the photo with the OCR that is already live. The keyboard never appears.
  • One action per screen. Camera has one shutter. Confirm has one green Send. Done has one OK.
  • Auto-tagged photo. Driver, truck, today's trip, GPS and time ride along with the photo. The driver chooses nothing.
  • Works with bad network. Photo queues on the phone and sends itself later. The driver is told so on the Done screen.
  • Call the office is one tap at the bottom of the home screen.
  • Tap targets are 64 px or taller. Text is 19–26 px on buttons. Green = send paper, amber = diesel, blue = my papers, red dot = something pending.

3 · What changes vs today's app
Today (DriverPortal.tsx) 3 tabs: Trips / Expenses / KYC · a map on the trip card · expense form with a dropdown of 8 types · typed Aadhaar, A/C number, IFSC fields · small dark text · 6 different photo buttons buried inside trip cards
This mock 3 big buttons on one screen · camera opens on tap · zero text fields · Hindi first · one photo flow shared by all three · status shown as colour + tick, not words · call office always visible

Rasta — trip se bill tak

  • 1Trip shuru — upar wala panel. Fixed / fooding / advance / doc / other, sab trip_id ke saath save. Bina trip ke koi kharch nahi.
  • 2Raste me — pump slip (HSD) aur FASTag (toll) apne register me aate hain, trip window se jud jaate hain. Galat lorry ka trip chuna → system mana karta hai (P0405).
  • 3Unloading — shortage KL aur uski penalty trip par likhi jaati hai, bill ke Shortage column me dikhti hai.
  • 41 aur 16 tareekh 03:00 — machine har owner ka 15-din ka bill draft banati hai (trip ke 7 column + freight).
  • 5Desk — Edit / Save; jo trip par darj nahi hua wo dashed box me likh sakte hain, wo bhi trip ke neeche hi jaata hai.
  • 6Admin Approve — voucher post, owner ke khaate me credit, bill lock, Print / WhatsApp / Email.

YES se pehle 4 baatein — inhi par build alag hoga

  1. Bill ek malik ka hai, ek lorry ka nahi. Sandeep ji ki 11 lorry ek bill me, IOCL ki tarah. Agar aap lorry-wise alag bill chahte hain to bolein — list me 11 row aayengi, foot ka hisaab lorry-wise.
  2. Trip Advance kharch ke side me hai. Attached lorry par advance humne owner ki taraf se diya, isliye bill me wapas kaata. Apni (OWN) gaadi par advance driver ke khaate me rehta hai, P&L me nahi.
  3. Commission aur TDS ka rate. 16 attached lorry me se kisi par rate darj nahi hai (master me 0 term). Upar 10% + 1% sirf dikhane ke liye. Ek baar batayein — sab par ek rate ya lorry-wise — main Commission Master me 1 Apr 2026 se bhar dunga.
  4. Toll Tax column abhi chhota dikhega. Apr se 3,883 FASTag crossing me sirf 493 trip se jude hain (₹2.40 L of ₹19.46 L). Build me ek "toll ko trip se jodo" kaam saath rakhna hoga, warna bill me toll 12% hi aayega.
